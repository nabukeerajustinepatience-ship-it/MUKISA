About Geofrey Farm

🌱 Our Story

Geofrey Farm was started by Ayinebyena Geofrey, a passionate farmer from Gayaaza, Uganda. What began as a small family farm has grown into a trusted source of fresh, quality farm products for the local community.

We believe in providing fresh, healthy, and affordable products directly from our farm to your table. No middlemen, no compromises - just pure Ugandan farming goodness.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide fresh, high-quality farm products to the people of Gayaaza and beyond while supporting sustainable farming practices and fair prices.

👁️ Our Vision

To become the most trusted farm in Uganda, known for quality products and excellent customer service.
